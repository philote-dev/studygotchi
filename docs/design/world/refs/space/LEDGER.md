# Reference ledger: space

Sky behind `/earth` and `/knowledge` (`SpaceField`, `SkySpiral` / `GeneratedPixelSpiral`, `spiral-galaxy.ts`, the cluster dust and constellation edges in `KnowledgeCanvas`). Reference use is for palette, structure and motion only: the galaxy stays procedurally generated (points, sprites, shaders), nothing here is ever sampled as a bitmap into the product. All files are public-domain NASA material or CC BY 4.0 ESA/Hubble, ESA/Webb and ESO releases; the credit lines below must travel with any reuse. Screensize JPEGs were fetched with `curl` on 2026-10-10; three NASA files were downscaled to 1600 px with `sips`. Companion canvas: `galaxy-and-black-hole-references.canvas.tsx`.
