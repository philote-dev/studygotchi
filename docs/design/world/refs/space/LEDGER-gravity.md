# Reference ledger: gravity objects (black hole, white hole, wormhole)

Companion to `LEDGER.md` for the `/lab/cosmos/gravity` exhibits (`frontend/components/lab/cosmos/gravity/**`). Same rules: palette, structure and motion only; nothing is sampled as a bitmap into the product. Files `g1`..`g3` were fetched with `curl` on 2026-10-11 as the publishers' screensize JPEGs (1280 px wide, nothing resized). Files `11`, `12`, `13`, `13b` in `LEDGER.md` remain the primary black-hole references.

| file | take | leave | date |
|---|---|---|---|
| [`g1-m87-jet-hubble.jpg`](g1-m87-jet-hubble.jpg) | The jet as a thin blue beam of knots on a warm yellow galaxy: one-sided, 1 to 2 cells wide at our scale, knots brighter near the base and spaced irregularly. The `jets` toggle draws two such beams (the counter-jet dimmer) as banded columns with knots that drift outward. | The host-galaxy glow and the star clusters; the exact 20 arcsec length (ours is 1.6 R). | 2026-10-11 |
| [`g2-m87-jet-and-shadow-gmva-eso.jpg`](g2-m87-jet-and-shadow-gmva-eso.jpg) | Ring and jet in one frame: the jet leaves the ring region as a wide, limb-brightened cone that narrows into two filaments. Proof that a jet reads as "belongs to the hole" when it starts at the ring, not at the shadow centre. Magenta-to-cream colour ramp is a good cool alternative palette. | The 3.5 mm blur and the wider ring at that wavelength. | 2026-10-11 |
| [`g3-m87-polarised-eht-eso.jpg`](g3-m87-polarised-eht-eso.jpg) | Polarisation streaks spiral around the ring (nearly azimuthal, wound slightly inward): the disc grain in the shader is a logarithmic spiral, `fract((phi + k log r) n / 2pi)`, not radial spokes, and it is densest on the bright southwest side. | The blur, and any attempt to show polarisation itself. | 2026-10-11 |

## Sources read (no file kept)

| topic | source | what it settled |
|---|---|---|
| Gargantua rendering | James, von Tunzelmann, Franklin, Thorne 2015, [CQG 32 065001](https://iopscience.iop.org/article/10.1088/0264-9381/32/6/065001) ([arXiv 1502.03808](https://arxiv.org/abs/1502.03808)); [DNEG summary](https://www.dneg.com/news/gravitational-lensing-by-spinning-black-holes); [SIGGRAPH 2015 talk](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/2775280.2792510) | Ray *bundles* through Kerr spacetime; the disc is lensed over and under the shadow; the film dropped Doppler colour and used spin a/M = 0.6 for legibility; the Einstein ring (image of a source behind the hole) is distinct from the photon ring. Our exhibit keeps brightness asymmetry, drops colour shift, draws the over-arc and under-arc as two flattened disc copies. |
| Schnittman visualisation | [NASA SVS 13326](https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/13326); [NASA feature](https://www.nasa.gov/universe/nasa-visualization-shows-a-black-holes-warped-world/); [SVS 14576 plunge](https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/14576/) | Far side of the disc appears as an arc over the top (and a smaller one below); the approaching (left) side is Doppler-brighter and the asymmetry vanishes face-on; the photon ring is a stack of thinner rings; the shadow is about twice the horizon (2.6 x in the Schwarzschild critical-impact-parameter sense, 3 sqrt(3)/2 r_s). Shadow 0.38 R / ring 0.42 R stays. |
| EHT images | [M87* polarisation, ApJL 910 L12](https://iopscience.iop.org/article/10.3847/2041-8213/abe71d); [Sgr A* polarisation, ApJL 964 L25](https://iopscience.iop.org/article/10.3847/2041-8213/ad2df0); [ESO eso2406](https://www.eso.org/public/news/eso2406/) | Azimuthal (spiral) polarisation pattern on the bright side, about 15 percent peak; both holes look alike, so one design covers both. |
| Jets | [ESO eso2305](https://www.eso.org/public/news/eso2305/); [ESA/Hubble opo0020a](https://esahubble.org/images/opo0020a/) | Jet base connects to the ring; one-sided in practice (counter-jet beamed away). |
| Feeding (TDE) | [STScI TDE concept](https://www.stsci.edu/contents/media/images/2024/204/01HWX7QJVQKXMJQ0M2B8NVYHCC); [phys.org on Price et al. 2024](https://phys.org/news/2024-08-star-destroyed-supermassive-black-hole.html) | A disrupted star becomes a thin stream that spirals in, brightens the disc and blows most of itself back out. The `feed` slider is a stream of 1-cell particles spiralling in along the disc plane plus a disc brightness lift; the `feed burst` beat adds a short spherical puff. |
| White holes | [physics.stackexchange 442824](https://physics.stackexchange.com/questions/442824/what-would-a-white-hole-look-like); [Chinese Physics C, accretion disk of a white hole](https://cpc.ihep.ac.cn/article/doi/10.1088/1674-1137/ad9899) | Time-reverse of a black hole: nothing can enter, everything inside must leave; it still deflects light like a mass. No credible imagery exists, so the design is the mirror of ours: bright banded core, outward particle fountain, inverted lens sign, cool palette. |
| Wormholes | Morris and Thorne 1988 ([Am. J. Phys. 56 395](https://www.pas.rochester.edu/~tim/introframe/AmJPhysBlackHoles.pdf)); [Muller 2004, "The image of a wormhole"](https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/physics/0505108); James et al. 2015, [Visualizing Interstellar's Wormhole](https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/1502.03809) | Catenoid embedding (z = b0 arcosh(r/b0)); Interstellar's "Dneg" wormhole is a sphere on the sky that shows the far celestial sphere inside, with lensing at the rim and an Einstein ring. Our mouth is a disc sprite sampling a second seeded star field through a barrel map, with the shared lens pass pulling the outer sky into a rim arc. |
| Cheap lensing | [erikjs, Bending light in a fragment shader](https://www.erikjs.com/blog/bending-light-in-a-fragment-shader); [s0xDk/ghostty-blackhole](https://github.com/s0xDk/ghostty-blackhole/blob/main/blackhole.glsl); [Radiant, How to render a black hole](https://radiant-shaders.com/learn/event-horizon); [TristanHottier/BH_Sim](https://github.com/TristanHottier/BH_Sim); [Tyler Kennedy, Godot black hole shader](https://tkte.ch/articles/2026/01/15/godot-blackhole-shader.html); [Shadertoy Gargantua, sonicether](https://www.shadertoy.com/view/lstSRS) | Thin-lens post pass (beta = theta - thetaE^2 / theta) is one texture fetch per pixel and good enough far from the hole; fade the field a few diameters out so the whole screen does not shimmer as the hole moves; damp the photon ring rather than supersample it. Geodesic marchers (150 to 900 steps) are not worth it under a 2 px pixel pass. |
| Games | [Mobius Digital, Brittle Hollow VFX](https://www.mobiusdigitalgames.com/news/brittle-hollow-visual-effects-vfx); [Super Mario Wiki, Launch Star](https://www.mariowiki.com/Launch_Star); [Super Mario Wiki, Black hole](https://www.mariowiki.com/Black_hole) | Screen-space warp sells a hole in a stylised world; a jet of particles marks "something fell in" (our feed burst). Launch Star: a round glowing door with a soft rim and a sparkle orbit is the cute wormhole read. |
